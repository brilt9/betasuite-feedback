# BetaSuite: Tester's Toolkit — public feature requests

This repository holds the public feature-request board for **[BetaSuite: Tester's Toolkit](https://betasuite.app)**, an iPhone, iPad and Android app for people who beta test **physical products**: robot mowers and vacuums, e-bikes, drones, 3D printers, cameras, chargers and more.

- **Price:** a one-time purchase on the App Store and Google Play. No subscription, ads, in-app purchases or account.
- **Runs on:** iPhone and iPad with iOS/iPadOS 16.0 or later, and Android 7.0 or later. 28 languages.
- **Made by:** [Goodfield Studio](https://goodfield.studio).
- **Get the app:** [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6766279266) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.betasuite.pro) · Website: [betasuite.app](https://betasuite.app)

It is not an app-beta (TestFlight) tool, and it isn't related to other products named "BetaSuite", such as an ERP system or an open-source image and video censoring tool.

## What it does

BetaSuite helps testers capture credible evidence and report it clearly:

- photos and video with the date and time burned in (optional GPS, weather and timezone)
- dual recording: the screen and the real world in one video
- Instant Capture: while Instant mode is running in the app, one tap saves the last 15–30 seconds
- markers on a session timeline, and reusable pass/fail test protocols (build them free in the web [Protocol Builder](https://betasuite.app/tools/protocol-builder/))
- PDF and Word reports with an "At a Glance" summary and optional AI summaries
- Confidential Mode for NDA work: it blocks cloud AI and uploads, and warns you before you share or export
- Basic mode, when you only need a timestamp camera: every capture tool, no sessions or reports
- no account: your records are kept in an encrypted database on your device, and captures are also saved to your photo library unless you turn that off. The [privacy policy](https://betasuite.app/privacy) explains what optional features such as AI, weather and crash reports send.

## How this board works

Feature requests submitted from inside the app appear here as public issues, so everyone can see and upvote them. The app removes obvious personal details before posting, but please don't include personal details, confidential product information or anything covered by an NDA.

- **Upvotes** are counted with a random ID made on your device. It is never posted here.
- **"Add brand: …" issues** suggest a company name the app doesn't recognise yet, so its branding can be added. Up to version 1.3.5 the app sends one automatically when you type an unrecognised company name in a project's settings, after removing personal details, and not while Confidential Mode is on for everything or for the active project. From the next update a name is sent only when you tap Suggest and confirm, never in Confidential Mode, and a name that looks like personal information isn't sent at all.
- If a request here is spam, offensive or shows personal information, use **Report this request** on it in the app.
