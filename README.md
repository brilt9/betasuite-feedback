# BetaSuite: Tester's Toolkit — public feature requests

This repository holds the public feature-request board for **[BetaSuite: Tester's Toolkit](https://betasuite.app)**, an iPhone, iPad and Android app for people who beta test **physical products**: robot mowers and vacuums, e-bikes, drones, 3D printers, cameras, chargers and more.

BetaSuite helps testers capture credible evidence and report it clearly:

- photos and video with the date and time burned in (optional GPS, weather and timezone)
- dual recording: the screen and the real world in one video
- markers on a session timeline, and reusable pass/fail test protocols
- PDF and Word reports with an "At a Glance" summary and optional AI summaries
- no account; your data stays on your device unless you share it
- Basic mode, when you only need a timestamp camera: every capture tool, no sessions or reports

It is not an app-beta (TestFlight) tool, and it isn't related to other products named "BetaSuite".

## How this board works

Feature requests submitted from inside the app appear here as issues, so everyone can see and upvote them. Please don't include personal details, confidential product information or anything covered by an NDA.
